# PSI-184 · Flow Boiling TSP — Flow Boiling Heat Transfer Using Temperature Sensitive Paints

- **Research area:** Fluid Physics
- **Sub-area:** Flow Boiling
- **Project type:** Ground Investigation
- **Keywords:** local heat flux · condensation · TSP · variable gravity · PSI grant

## Objective

Obtain spatiotemporal data in Zero-G flights during up-flow and down-flow in varying gravities (lunar, martian, 0-g) to obtain heat transfer coefficients along the tube.

## Research impacts

The flight data is being used to develop and validate sub-models of the flow within each flow regime. Understanding of the heat transfer mechanisms will enable space-based heat exchangers to be designed with much more confidence. Although this work is primarily aimed at thermal management subsystems and development of lightweight evaporators, it also impacts other systems for novel power and transmission, advanced chemical propulsion, and energy storage.
