Model assumptions

Airflow: incompressible stable-fluids solver (semi-Lagrangian advection + Jacobi pressure projection) on a ~0.14 m grid bounded by your capsule's CAD profile.

Smoke, gas temperature and O₂ depletion are passive scalars with explicit diffusion. Buoyancy acceleration = 9.81·g·β·T with a fixed visualization gain β.

Flame intensity scales with local ppO₂ / 21.3 kPa and dims as the model depletes O₂ around it. Source strengths, β, diffusivity and the extinction threshold are visualization constants, not PSI data.

Only values tagged with a PSI accession come from base_de_datos.
